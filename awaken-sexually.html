What binaural audio actually is

Two slightly different frequencies, one in each ear. Your brain resolves the difference as a third tone that isn't in either channel, and tracking that tone pulls your brainwaves toward the same rate. That's the whole mechanism. It's physics and it's about eighty years old.

Two things follow from that. Headphones are not optional — through a speaker the two channels merge in the air and the effect doesn't happen. And you don't have to believe anything for it to work, which is most of why I like it.

What it does is put you under without asking you to get there yourself. Useful when you want to feel something and have no energy to go and find it.

The things you're already thinking

Do I need special headphones?

No. Any stereo headphones or earbuds. The only requirement is that your left ear and right ear hear different channels — so not a speaker, and not one earbud.

Is it safe to fall asleep to?

Yes, and most people do. If you have a seizure disorder, check with your doctor first — that's the standard caution for anything rhythmic, and I'd rather say it than not.
