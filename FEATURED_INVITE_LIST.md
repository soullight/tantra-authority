## Editorial-standards blurb (for the submissions page)

> **Our editorial standard.**
> *Tantra Authority* features and publishes real, verifiable people who do real work. Three things are non-negotiable, for everyone we feature and everyone we list:
>
> **1. Real names, real backgrounds.** No anonymous "sex coach" branding, no invented credentials. (Working artists may use an established professional moniker, disclosed as such.)
>
> **2. Lineage in the open.** Tell us who you trained with, what schools and traditions shaped you — and if there's a cult association anywhere in your lineage, name it. We disclose it at the byline; we do not bury it. Disclosure keeps you eligible. Concealment doesn't.
>
> **3. Safety is the precondition.** We do not feature shame-based teaching, cult-style hierarchies, coercion, or undisclosed harm. Hidden pricing, "let's discuss in DMs," and "DM me to apply" are disqualifying. We can and do remove anyone if credible reports of harm emerge or lineage claims prove false. No appeal process — the integrity of these pages is the product.
>
> We are body-positive and mechanism-honest, not sex-negative and not sex-positive in the cheerleading sense. We render every subject as a full human being. If that's the company you want to keep, we'd like to hear from you.
>
> Featured submissions: **editorial@tantraauthority.com** (mark "FEATURED"). Gallery: **gallery@tantraauthority.com**. Directory: **directory@tantraauthority.com**.
