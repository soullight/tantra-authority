## 2. Editorial voice — the rules every published word must follow

The voice spec lives in the brain memory at `feedback_substrate_stays_invisible.md` and in the PILLARS document. Operational summary:

**The voice IS:**
- Calm, authoritative, body-grounded
- Anti-bullshit without being snarky
- Em-dash heavy (this is a stylistic signature, not a flaw)
- Direct. Names things. Avoids hedging.
- Uses "the body" as a working term — not "your body" in a coaching-coddle way
- NYC-street undercurrent. Lawrence is a NYC street kid; the street stays in
- Real names where useful (Vilayanur Ramachandran, Justin Lehmiller, Brad Sagarin, Kasia Urbaniak, Janet Hardy, etc.). Specificity earns trust.
- Real numbers where they exist (4-8% for foot fetish, 40-65% for submission, etc.)
- Reframes from a position of seeing — never moralizing, never pitying

**The voice IS NOT:**
- First-person memoir prose ("I once" / "in my experience"). This is the Tantra Authority voice, not Lawrence's personal Substack voice. First-person belongs at The Naked Mind.
- Therapy-frame language ("trauma-informed", "hold space for", "honor your journey"). Vomits.
- New Age vocabulary ("manifest", "vibration", "energetic activation", "high-frequency", "twin flame"). All banned.
- MFA-precious literary fiction prose. Not a Granta essay.
- Pity. Pity is hierarchical. Compassion is horizontal. We do compassion.
- "Yass queen" sex-positive cheerleading.
- Soft hedges ("perhaps", "maybe", "it might be that"). Cut them.
- Coaching-style direct address ("you should", "try this") more than necessary. Articles describe the work; readers do it.
- Substrate-naming. Never name Game 4, Principle 67, OH SHIT shapes, the Slipstream, etc. *in articles.* Those frameworks shape HOW we write. They are not the topic. The topic is always real life — stories, real moments, encounters, mechanisms.
- Self-promotion as the lede. Books / coaching / matching are CTAs at the end, not the opening pitch.

**Forbidden constructions:**
- "We don't sell weekend retreats" / "we don't certify" — the dogmatic anti-monetization stance was removed. Charging is part of the work.
- "Sex workers" in the practitioner directory copy — say "tantra teachers, somatic guides, breathwork facilitators, erotic-hypnosis practitioners, body-workers, ritualists" instead. The site does not list sex workers. It can write *about* sex work editorially.
- "Lawrence Daniels" or "Lawrence Gregory" — the byline is **Lawrence Lanoff**. Locked.
- "Imprint of LK Publishing, LLC" footer noise — stripped from all pages.

**The "Invite the Animal In" close** is non-negotiable. It is the tonal flip from sober-editor to wild-erotic-alive. Permission for the reader to take the framework into the body. 1-3 sentences max in the headline + intro before the rabbit-holes. Wild but not unhinged. The opposite of clinical.

**Rabbit-holes** point at: related Shadow Library articles (linked or marked forthcoming), the relevant book volume, the directory, the matching service, the test, the shop (specific section), the newsletter, hypnosis recordings, coaching. Mix in-site (linked) with forthcoming (italicized, "coming") to seed demand.

## 5. Article submission and contributor vetting

For external contributors (the site is currently mostly Lawrence + the Tantra Authority Editors, but will open to vetted contributors).

**Vetting requirements for contributors:**
1. **Real name.** No pen names except in unusual circumstances cleared with editorial.
2. **Lineage disclosure.** Who they trained with, what schools, what teachers, what cult associations (if any). Disclosed at the byline level, not buried.
3. **Sample of prior writing.** Either published or unpublished, but a recognizable voice.
4. **Voice audit on a draft.** New contributors submit one draft for editorial pass before any publication. The audit checks: substrate stays invisible, no therapy-frame, no New Age vocabulary, no first-person Lawrence-voice impersonation, em-dash style consistent.
5. **Topic fit.** Articles must serve one of the five categories above. Off-topic submissions get redirected, not published.

**Submissions inbox:** `editorial@tantraauthority.com` (intake form to follow once forms infrastructure is wired up).

**Editorial decisions are final.** No appeal process. Bad fits don't ship.

## 6. Practitioner directory — vetting standards

Listed practitioners must satisfy ALL of the following:

1. **Real name + real face** in the listing. No pseudonymous practitioners.
2. **Lineage disclosed.** Teachers, schools, training programs. If there is a cult association in their lineage, it is named — not omitted.
3. **Five years minimum** of paid practice in their stated lane. Not negotiable.
4. **No active complaints** from former clients that the practitioner cannot account for.
5. **Modality fit:** tantra teachers, somatic guides, breathwork facilitators, erotic-hypnosis practitioners, body-workers, ritualists, erotic-wellness coaches, erotic photographers and artists. **The directory does not list sex workers, escorts, or transactional intimate-services providers.** That category carries FOSTA/regulatory risk we are not absorbing. We can write *about* the territory editorially; we cannot host the listings.
6. **Self-disclosure of session structure and pricing.** Hidden pricing or "let's discuss in DMs" is a red flag we treat as disqualifying.

**Removal:** Listed practitioners can be removed at editorial discretion if (a) credible reports of harm emerge, (b) lineage representations were false, (c) they take public positions incompatible with the safety pillar (shame-based teaching, cult-style hierarchy, etc.), (d) they fall out of active practice. No appeal process. The integrity of the directory is the product.

**Submissions inbox:** `directory@tantraauthority.com`.

## 7. Featured spreads — editorial standards

Each Featured spread (Woman, Fetish, Artist, Photographer) follows a fixed format and serves the integrated-woman strategic spine.

**Featured Woman** — the cover feature. Long-form interview (3,000-6,000 words). Real editorial photography (curated, full-resolution, dignifying). Full lineage and work disclosure. The subject speaks; her work is documented; her body is photographed honestly; the reader leaves understanding her, not consuming her. The format is closer to the 1970s Playboy Interview than to current-era objectification, with a contemporary art-magazine sensibility on the imagery.

**Featured Fetish** — one specific fetish per issue, in cooperation with a Shadow Library article on the same topic. Practitioner / artist / writer in that lane is showcased alongside.

**Featured Artist** — erotic art, ritual art, somatic art, performance. Portfolio + interview + shop access.

**Featured Photographer** — same model, narrowed to photography. Lawrence Lanoff's own photography rotates in.

**Standards across all four:**
- Subjects own their material. Image rights, interview transcripts, and exit clauses all go to the subject. No surprise re-use.
- No surprises in publication. Subjects review the full piece before it ships.
- Lineage transparent. Cult associations disclosed.
- The work is shown alongside the person — never the person reduced to the work, never the work reduced to the person.

## 11. Crisis response

**If credible report of harm by a listed practitioner arrives:**
1. Listing temporarily hidden within 24 hours of report.
2. Editorial review, including outreach to practitioner for response, within 7 days.
3. Decision (reinstate, permanent removal, or further investigation) within 14 days.
4. If removal: practitioner is informed by email; no public statement unless multiple corroborating reports indicate broader risk to readers.

**If legal threat (cease-and-desist, libel claim) arrives:**
1. Do not respond directly. Forward immediately to retained counsel.
2. Preserve all relevant records.
3. Continue normal operations unless counsel advises otherwise.
4. If counsel determines the editorial position is defensible (truth, opinion, fair comment), defend it. If it is indefensible, retract and apologize. Operate from accuracy, not from fear of being challenged.

**If a contributor or subject discloses harm during reporting:**
1. Do not pretend to be a clinician.
2. Provide the relevant referral resources (RAINN for sexual assault, 988 for crisis, the Tantra Authority directory for somatic practitioners).
3. Continue the editorial relationship with care for the disclosure but do not let the disclosure become the editorial frame unless the subject explicitly invites that frame.

**If publication of an article triggers a public backlash:**
1. Distinguish: is the criticism substantive (a factual or framework error) or reactive (it offends a constituency that the article was not written for)?
2. Substantive criticism = correct or amend the piece, publicly.
3. Reactive criticism = let it be. The site is not written for the offended constituency. Engagement-baiting backlashes by responding to them.
4. Never apologize for accurate, well-framed work. Never defend inaccurate or sloppy work. The line is accuracy, not popularity.

- "Goddess" or "god" used as flattery for a person

## 16. Definitions of done

For any piece of work shipped under the Tantra Authority name:

**An article is done when:**
- It uses the standard structure (article-meta → h1 → body sections → bigger-picture close → animal-cta → rabbit-holes → footer)
- The voice audit passes (no first-person except in quotes, no New Age, no therapy-frame, no substrate-naming, em-dash style consistent)
- It serves the strategic spine (one of the three load-bearing facts is recognizable in the framing)
- It has been read aloud once before publishing (this is the cleanest voice check)
- All internal links resolve
- The "Test" link is in the nav
- The footer reads *Tantra Authority — published by The Naked Press*

**A directory listing is done when:**
- All vetting requirements (section 6) are documented in the listing record
- Real name, real face, lineage, pricing, modality are visible in the public listing
- The practitioner has reviewed and approved the listing copy
- Removal protocol is acknowledged in the contributor agreement

- The subject has approved the full piece pre-publication
- Image rights, interview transcript, exit clauses are documented
- The piece serves the integrated-woman strategic spine
- It includes both portrait-of-person and portrait-of-work components

- It passes the safety-pillar test (does this surface generate or undermine the safety condition?)
