What energy sex actually is

It's the difference between looking at the ocean through glass and surfing in it. Sex is communication, movement, foreplay and feeling connected. The energy is how all that gets felt, and you can learn to feel it with or without touch.

It's ordinary sensing

There's nothing magical about it. It's the same sensing you use when you're listening in the dark, or trying to feel something you can't quite make out. It's a very open kind of listening. In the video I'm not even touching Leah. I'm tuning in, moving my hand along the line of her body, and watching her body respond and open.

The more relaxed the body is, the more pleasure it can feel. Energy work is mostly a way of getting there.

Start alone: feel your own field

You can't share a field you've never felt. Sit somewhere quiet, breathe slowly into your belly with a long exhale, and put your attention on your skin as a whole: where the air meets you, the temperature, the clothes. Stay with that a few minutes.

Then let your attention go an inch or two past your skin. Most people feel almost nothing the first time. Some feel a tingle, warmth or a little pressure. Keep at it for ten or fifteen minutes and the line between inside and outside softens. That zone is what I call the field.

Then with a partner

Sit facing each other, knees nearly touching. Each of you does the solo practice first. Then open your eyes and let your attention move into the space between you. Don't push and don't pull. Just breathe and stay. After a while it stops feeling like two separate fields. That's the doorway everything in the video comes through.
