1. The two kisses to stop doing

Leah calls them kissing traumas, and everyone has had one. The sword fight: no lips, just a fast, pointed tongue. And the ambush: he's excited, he comes straight in, tongue first, no movement, and she feels pinned. If you recognize yourself in either one, good. Now you know what to drop.

2. Get out of your head

Kissing isn't hard. The hard part is getting out of your head. A kiss happens in your body, moment by moment. The minute it has a goal (where this is going, what happens next), you stop feeling it and start managing it. She can tell.

3. Nervous? Slow down

Almost everyone speeds up when they're nervous. Do the opposite. Take a breath. Feel what's happening in your body and in hers. Slower is almost always better, and it reads as confidence even when you don't feel any.

4. Lips lead, tongue follows

Use less tongue. Let your lips do the leading and the following: light lips on lips, then notice what happens. Keep everything relaxed: lips, tongue, jaw. Taste her lips before anything else. More will come when the momentum is there, and you'll both feel when it is.

The whole lesson in one line: relaxed, slow, and paying attention beats any technique.

5. Where kissing goes from here

A kiss is the smallest version of everything else. The same three things (getting out of your head, slowing down, staying relaxed) are what make the rest of sex go well too. Most men were never taught any of it. That's what my course is for.
