The short answer

Tantra is a set of practices that use breath, attention and the body to wake you up to the present moment, including in sex. Most of what you've heard (robes, gurus, hours-long sex, secret energies) is mythology layered on top. Take that away and what's left is a skill you can learn.

What tantra is not

  • Not a religion you have to join
  • Not only about sex, and not about performing
  • Not something you need a guru, or a retreat in Bali, to start
