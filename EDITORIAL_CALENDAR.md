## Pacing

Target cadence per OPERATIONAL_PROTOCOLS.md: **1 substantive article per week**, rotating across categories. At that pace this backlog runs ~18 months without seed-level pieces; with seed-level expansion, 3+ years of material.

Recommended rotation:
- Week 1: Practice (female-orgasm-liberation cluster)
- Week 2: Foundations or Shadow Library (depth piece)
- Week 3: Practice (male-sexuality cluster)
- Week 4: Shadow Library (Beyond the Myth)
- Repeat with adjusted clusters

Featured spreads (Woman / Fetish / Artist / Photographer) run on a parallel monthly cadence and intersect with whichever Shadow Library piece publishes in the same window.

## How to use this calendar

1. Pick the next article from QUEUED. Prefer high-search-volume topics during early growth phase.
2. Confirm it doesn't conflict with the parallel-agent's work (`git pull` first).
3. Outline the article — usually 6-10 sections matching the article-structure spec.
4. Draft, voice-audit per OPERATIONAL_PROTOCOLS.md §13.
5. Update status in this file from QUEUED → LIVE on commit.
6. Add the article-card to `index.html`.
7. Cross-link from related LIVE articles' rabbit-holes.
8. Commit and push.

The Test link is in the nav across all pages — every new article inherits it via the standard header block.
