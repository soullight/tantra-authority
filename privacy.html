Privacy

Plain language, no fine-print games. This covers the psychosexual test and anything else you send us.

What we collect

  • Your email (so we can send your result code).
  • Your test answers and the profile they generate.
  • Optionally, your gender (only to show gender-specific questions).

That's it. We don't ask for your real name, and you never have to give it.

How it's handled — this is the part that matters

  • Stored encrypted in a private database. Not public, not indexable, not searchable.
  • Never sold. Never shared with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone.
  • Never shown publicly and never attached to your name anywhere.
  • Your sexual answers are treated as exactly what they are — the most sensitive data there is.

What we use it for

  • To send you your result code.
  • Only if you choose it — to match you when the compatibility service launches.
  • To understand, in aggregate and anonymized, what people are working with — never tied to you individually.

Your control

Want your data deleted? Email us and it's gone — all of it, permanently. No questions, no hoops.

The basics

  • Adults only (18+).
  • We use Cloudflare to run the site and a payment processor (Clover) only if you buy something — they never see your test answers.
  • This isn't therapy or medical care.
